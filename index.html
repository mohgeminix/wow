Connect the scanner (one time)

  1. Turn the scanner on (keyboard / HID mode — most are already).
  2. Phone or tablet: Settings → Bluetooth → pair the scanner.
  3. Open this app. Scan any product — it appears by itself.

Install as an app (one time)

  1. Android (Chrome): menu ⋮ → Install app.
  2. iPhone/iPad (Safari): Share □↑ → Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open it from the Gemini X icon — full screen, works without internet.
