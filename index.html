How it saves

Every sale is saved by itself on this device the moment you press Add (see the ✓ counter on top). On the last day of each month press Export and send the Excel file to Gemini X. Do not clear the browser data.

Send sales to Gemini X

This button exports the month that is open in the ▤ Month tab — its name and number of sales are written on the button. On the last day of each month: press it, then send the Excel file to Gemini X.

Safety copy

Backup puts every sale of every month into one small file — send it to WhatsApp or keep it on a computer. If this browser is ever cleaned, Restore brings all sales back from that file.
